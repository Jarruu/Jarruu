## System Overview

Built a structured financial reporting web system for BEM KM STIA Adabiah to make student organization bookkeeping transparent, orderly, and easy to audit.

The platform organizes incoming funds and outgoing expenditures into clear periodic summaries so board members and stakeholders can review financial standing without sifting through scattered manual records.

## Key Capabilities & Workflow

- **Structured Financial Management:** Organizes student organization financial records in a clear, transparent digital format.
- **Income & Expense Processing:** Records and categorizes cash inflows and outflows to support organizational accountability.
- **Periodic Financial Recaps:** Generates periodic financial summaries designed to be readily understood by officers and stakeholders.
- **Administrative Efficiency:** Replaces fragmented manual bookkeeping with an orderly, faster recording workflow.
